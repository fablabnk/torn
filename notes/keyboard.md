Cherry MX Hotswap switches do not need to be soldered to a PCB. Hotswap PCBs are designed to allow switches to be inserted and removed without soldering, thanks to the use of hotswap sockets like Kailh or Mill-Max. These sockets have spring-loaded contacts that make electrical connections with the switch pins when inserted, allowing for easy swapping of switches without the need for soldering
